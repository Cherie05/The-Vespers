### Slide 1: Title & Hook
- **Title:** VesperAero — Federated Climate Forensics & Cross-Border Plume Surveillance
- **Sub-tagline:** Turning citizens into hyper-local pollution sensors across BRICS economic corridors.
- **Team & Event:** Arun & Co | Build with AI 2026 (Sustainability Pillar)

### Slide 2: The Macro-Air Blindspot (Problem)
- Macro monitoring stations in major cities miss **hyper-local emission spikes** (illegal stack flaring, brick kilns, farm residue burning).
- Emissions do not respect administrative borders: trans-boundary smog causes diplomatic paralysis and public health emergencies.
- Lack of ground-truthed, real-time forensic telemetry for rapid enforcement.

### Slide 3: The Solution — VesperAero
- A federated, citizen-augmented environmental intelligence grid.
- **The Core Loop:** Citizen Capture (GPS + Photo + Voice) $\to$ Contextualize (Open-Meteo Wind) $\to$ Google Gemini AI Vision Forensics $\to$ Atmospheric Gaussian Plume Dispersion Model $\to$ NASA FIRMS Satellite Correlation $\to$ Inter-Agency Dispatch.

### Slide 4: Google AI Integration (The Core Engine)
- **Model:** Google Gemini 1.5/2.5 Flash Vision Multimodal API.
- **Input:** Raw citizen imagery + live meteorological wind vector + voice observation note.
- **Output:** Structured Forensic Classification, Visual Density Score (1–10 based on optical opacity), Detected Pollutant Compounds (PM2.5, SO2, NOx, Black Carbon), Trans-Boundary Risk Vector, and Actionable Enforcement Recommendations.

### Slide 5: Atmospheric Physics: Gaussian Plume Modeling
- Combining Gemini's density estimate $Q$ with Pasquill-Gifford atmospheric dispersion equations.
- Computes lateral turbulent dispersion $\sigma_y(x)$ and downwind transport velocity $u$.
- *Judges note:* Explicitly framed as a calibrated estimation model designed to integrate with Continuous Emission Monitoring Systems (CEMS) in production.

### Slide 6: Satellite Ground Truthing (NASA FIRMS)
- Integrates live NASA MODIS & VIIRS thermal anomaly satellite data.
- Instant cross-verification between citizen reports and orbital thermal signatures (Fire Radiative Power in MW and brightness temperature in K).

### Slide 7: Detection Depth & Hotspot Clustering
- Dynamic multi-report clustering heatmap moves beyond one-off complaints to discover genuine persistent pollution hotspots.
- Correlates repeat industrial breaches and seasonal agricultural burning patterns.

### Slide 8: Cross-Border Applicability & BRICS Federation
- Standardized `BRICS-AERO-FED-v1.0` JSON telemetry schema for cross-border data exchange.
- Demonstrated across key BRICS corridors:
  1. Punjab Agricultural Corridor (India ⇄ Pakistan)
  2. Amazon Agro-Frontier (Brazil ⇄ Bolivia / Paraguay)
  3. Mpumalanga Highveld Mining/Smelting Belt (South Africa ⇄ Mozambique)
  4. Yangtze Maritime & Industrial Zone (China)

### Slide 9: Multilingual & Voice-First Inclusivity
- Accessible to grassroots farmers, industrial workers, and urban citizens.
- Native UI in 5 BRICS languages: English, हिन्दी, Português, Русский, 中文.
- Integrated Web Speech API for voice-driven observations.

### Slide 10: 100% Free-Tier & Zero-Friction Deployability
- **Frontend:** React + Vite on Netlify (instant edge CDN).
- **Backend:** Node.js + Express on Render (zero card required).
- **Mapping:** Leaflet.js + OpenStreetMap (no billing account needed).
- **Data:** Open-Meteo + NASA FIRMS + Firebase Spark / In-Memory resilient store.
- Ready to be piloted within a municipal environment department in hours.

### Slide 11: Impact & Scalability Roadmap
- **Week 1–4:** Pilot in high-density border district with local citizen sensors and pollution control board.
- **Month 2–6:** Integration with state CEMS ground sensors and drone verification squads.
- **Month 6+:** Inter-governmental BRICS air quality pact data hub.
